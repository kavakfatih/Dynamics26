## 1. Why this plan exists

Dynamics26 completed the V1.1.0-rc.1 UI/UX/GUI hardening line and froze UI feature work except for
engineering blockers and serious defects.

The next product-critical capability is the original Dynamics26 meshing engine. During the meshing
research program, several R&D packages were investigated deeply and some executable research evidence
was written ahead of the production dependency chain. This plan separates those two concepts:

- `M0..M10` = research/work-package taxonomy,
- `DEV-MESH-P0..P7` = production development order.

An R&D package being designed, frozen or prototyped does **not** mean the corresponding production
milestone is complete.

## 12. Research continues during development

The project remains research-first, but research becomes **just in time** rather than a separate long
pre-development block.

For every DEV-MESH subphase:

```text
Current implementation question
-> targeted research delta
-> source/benchmark update
-> Dynamics26 decision / ADR if needed
-> independent oracle or prototype
-> production implementation
-> qualification
-> regression
-> exact-head macOS CI
```

Rules:

- do not silently change a frozen contract,
- new evidence may supersede a frozen decision only through an explicit ADR,
- research prototypes may be discarded if they do not fit the qualified architecture,
- commercial/open-source behavior remains benchmark evidence, not implementation truth,
- development is allowed to uncover new research questions.
